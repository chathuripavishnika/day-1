Flowers

Rose Flowers

The rose is a beautiful and popular flower. It comes in different colours, such as red, pink, white, and yellow. Roses are symbols of love, beauty, and friendship. They have a sweet fragrance and soft petals. People give roses as gifts on birthdays, Valentine's Day, and other special occasions.

Butterfly Pea flower

The butterfly pea flower is a beautiful flower that is usually blue in colour. It is also known as the blue pea flower. This flower grows on a climbing plant. It is commonly found in Sri Lanka and other tropical countries. People use this flower to make natural blue tea. It is also used as a natural food colouring. The butterfly pea flower is special because of its bright colour and natural beauty.

Sun Flowers

The sunflower is a beautiful flower. It is usually yellow in colour with a large round centre. Sunflowers need sunlight and water to grow well. They are commonly found in gardens and farms. Sunflowers produce seeds that are used to make sunflower oil. They are also loved by bees and butterflies. The sunflower is a symbol of happiness, love, and positivity.
