Animals

Four-Legged animals

Four-legged animals are animals that have four legs. Examples of four-legged animals are dogs, cats, cows, goats, horses, elephants, lions, and tigers. Some of these animals live with humans, while others live in forests. Cows and goats provide us with milk. Dogs protect our homes, and horses help people with transportation. Elephants are large and strong animals. These animals are important to nature and humans. We should love and protect all animals.

important

Animals are very important to our lives and the environment. They help to maintain the balance of nature. Some animals provide us with milk, eggs, meat, and wool. Dogs protect our homes, and cows provide us with milk. Bees and butterflies help flowers to grow by pollination. Some animals help farmers with their work. Wild animals are also important for maintaining the food chain. Animals make our world more beautiful and interesting. Therefore, we should love, protect, and care for all animals.
